# The Wizard Flow

1. **Consents** — GDPR + policy + keep-me-updated. Creates the submission (UUID, IP, timestamps).
2. **Bill Upload** — Optional multi-page upload. AI extraction produces editable prefilled data. Dropped from the steps when document upload is off.
3. **Personal Data** — Name, email, phone, CF, POD (validated, prefilled from extraction) + manifest extra fields. Optional ID card upload with cross-validation against bill data. With document upload off, the manual fields only.

Document upload and scanning are what `GET /api/{rec}/config` reports in `features.document_upload` and `features.document_scan`; the wizard reads them and never infers availability. They are off unless the deployment sets both `EXTRACTION_ENABLED` and `EXTRACTION_API_KEY`, because scanning sends identity documents to the extraction endpoint. Off, the feature is absent rather than disabled: no upload control, no placeholder, and no step requires a document.
4. **Energy System** — PV, battery, EV, heat pump questions (manifest-driven, with conditional visibility).
5. **Eligibility** — Address geocoded and checked against coverage rules (if configured). For a template that declares its areas as primary-substation boundaries, the geocoded point is sent to the Digital Twin's `boundary_at_point` with this service's own token, and the boundary covering it decides both eligibility and the member's area: eligible only if it is one of the template's areas, with no fallback to a municipality. The anonymous answer says yes or no and nothing about where (no boundary id, no area, no matched rule). A Digital Twin that does not answer is a 503 — "we cannot check your address right now" — never a yes or a no, and nothing is kept. The route is rate-limited per client address (`RATE_LIMIT_ELIGIBILITY`, `429` over it). See [specifications/eligibility-and-areas.md](specifications/eligibility-and-areas.md).
6. **Statute** — Separate consent for community statute. Also collects **optional data-sharing consent** when the manifest declares `consent.data_sharing`: offers are rendered from `GET {DS_NS_URL}/ns/sharing-offers` (consent-based offers show a toggle; contract-based offers are disclosed without one), and the SHA-256 of the exact consent text shown is recorded. Placed here — not in the consents step, which runs before any data is collected and would be uninformed consent.
7. **Review** — Summary of all entered data. Submit triggers PDF generation + email notification.

**Where the supply point is, for a template with boundaries.** The submission records `supply_boundary_id` and `supply_boundary_source`, and nothing else about where: never the coordinates. The server resolves them from the supply address the submission holds — the address the eligibility step checked, which the wizard saves as `supply_address` (`{"text": …}`, encrypted), or, when there is none, the scanned `extracted_data.indirizzo` — on a save that changes that address, on submit, and again at approval, and never from a client: no request field sets them, and a value a client sends is dropped. A save whose check cannot be made records no id and still saves; a submit is refused with `422` when there is no supply address or it falls in no boundary of the template's areas, and with `503` when the geocoder or the Digital Twin does not answer. The applicant's own read of the submission does not carry the boundary; the operator console does. With document scanning off, the checked address is the only supply address the submission holds, and it is enough: a boundary community can be submitted and approved without scanning (REQ-0018).

Data-sharing consent is **optional** (GDPR Art. 7(4)): it is never required and never blocks `can_submit()`. On the submission it records `data_sharing_consent`, `data_sharing_consent_at`, `data_sharing_consent_offer_ids`, `data_sharing_consent_text_version`, `data_sharing_consent_locale`, `data_sharing_consent_text_sha256`, `data_sharing_offers_presented` (every consent-based offer the step showed, accepted or not, as id and version — checked against the published offers), and `share_provisioned` (whether the consent was pushed to the connector). On approval these offers are provisioned to the dataspace connector — see [data-sharing.md](data-sharing.md) and [dataspace-integration.md](dataspace-integration.md).
